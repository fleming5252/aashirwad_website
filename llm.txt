## About

Aashirwad Institute of Teachers was established in 2009 and is headquartered in Vaikom, Kottayam, Kerala. The About page describes it as an "Academy for Competitive Examinations, Research & Training" and as a comprehensive educational ecosystem.

Educational focus stated on the About page:

* Competitive examinations
* Teacher eligibility training
* School education
* Civil service preparation
* Digital learning
* Skill development

Examinations and training areas explicitly mentioned:

* Kerala PSC
* KTET
* CTET
* Navodaya Entrance
* Civil Services
* SSC
* Other national and state-level examinations

Academic approach listed on the page: conceptual clarity, systematic preparation, regular assessment, quality learning resources, personalised mentoring, and continuous academic support.

The Civil Service Nurture Programme (CSNP) is described as a flagship initiative that guides aspirants progressively from school level towards the Civil Services, covering knowledge, analytical thinking, communication skills, current affairs awareness, confidence and leadership qualities.

Digital initiatives named on the page are Aashirwad Rank File and Aashirwad EduVerse, which provide study materials, current affairs, multimedia resources and interactive learning tools alongside conventional classroom learning.

Figures stated by the website on its own About page (self-reported, not independently verified): 23 first ranks in Kerala PSC examinations, and since 2010 a record in Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya admissions from Kottayam. The page also describes a KTET success commitment under which eligible candidates who do not qualify after completing the prescribed training may attend the next training session free of charge, subject to the applicable programme terms and conditions.

The Courses page lists nine courses. Descriptions below reflect the visible page text.

1. Women Police — Specialized coaching for Women Police constable exams with physical training and mock interviews. Category: Kerala Police recruitment / Women Police Constable.
2. Police Constable — Complete preparation for Kerala Police Constable exams including written, physical, and viva. Category: Kerala Police recruitment.
3. LGS (Lower Grade) — Thorough coaching for Lower Grade Servants exams with focus on Kerala PSC syllabus. Category: Kerala PSC.
4. LDC Clerk — Expert-led coaching for Lower Division Clerk exams with comprehensive study materials. Category: Kerala PSC.
5. Degree Level — Degree-level PSC exam preparation covering all subjects with expert faculty guidance. Category: Kerala PSC.
6. Secretariat Assistant — Specialized coaching for Secretariat Assistant exams with focus on Kerala PSC standards. Category: Kerala PSC.

8. CTET — Central Teacher Eligibility Test preparation with all subjects covered by expert educators. Category: CTET (Central Teacher Eligibility Test).
9. Civil Service Foundation — Foundation course for Civil Services with comprehensive coverage of prelims and mains syllabus. Category: Civil Services.

## App

Aashirwad EduVerse is the institute's mobile application for Kerala PSC and competitive examination preparation, available on the Google Play Store and the Apple App Store.

* Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=dev.fle.ashirwad&hl=en_IN
* Apple App Store: https://apps.apple.com/in/app/aashirwad-eduverse/id1501952592

Learning modes described on the page: Reading Mode, Writing Mode, Audio Mode, and Video Mode.

Features described on the page:

* Choose Your Exam — preparation is organised separately by chosen competitive exam.
* Syllabus Tree — digital materials organised module-wise into subjects, topics, subtopics and chapters.
* Keyword Search — full-text and keyword search across content by subject and topic.
* Smart Exam Modes & Live Contests — unlimited practice exams, test series, previous year papers, mock exams, and live contests with leaderboards.
* Daily Current Affairs & Editorial Insights — curated current affairs with visual aids and editorials on major global and national events.
* The Educational Game Zone — quizzes and short educational activities intended to track daily consistency.
* Progress tracking across the learning journey.

The FAQ section states that a 2-day free trial is available, and that the institute also provides offline training alongside the digital app.
